Conference Schedule and Breakout Sessions

The Keynote Address and Breakout Sessions were designed using the L.E.A.D. Framework, reflecting Westminster's commitment to purposeful innovation. We do not pursue technology for its own sake. We learn what is changing, equip educators with practical tools, aspire toward stronger learning experiences, and disciple students with wisdom, integrity, and Christ-centered purpose.
